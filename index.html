FAQ

Questions students usually ask.

Who is the training designed for?

It is designed for engineering students and freshers who want practical exposure to software, cloud, DevOps and AI technologies. Colleges can also run it as a program for their students.

Can a college run this program for its students?

Yes. Sky Forge Solutions delivers the training at the college, aligned with the academic calendar. Request a proposal and we will share the details.

How are students placed into tracks?

An assessment places each student in the Python, Agentic AI or DevOps track, and regular exams with feedback track their progress.

Is the learning practical?

Yes. The curriculum emphasizes hands-on exercises, automation, workflows and project-based learning.

Do students work on projects?

Yes. Project work is used to bring together the concepts learned across the technology tracks.

Is career guidance included?

Career-focused support includes profile optimization, GitHub portfolio guidance, interview preparation and placement assistance. Placement assistance is an effort to help students prepare and connect with opportunities; it is not a guarantee of a job.
